# Historical experiments

These assets were retained while separating maintained examples from research
history. Former root-level demonstrators now live here under their original
names. `legacy/` contains the former add-in `Files/` and `exampleParts/` folders.

Nothing in this directory is required to compile the add-in. Logs, screenshots,
documents and CAD data remain available for reproducing earlier experiments;
historical scripts or documents may still refer to their former absolute paths.
